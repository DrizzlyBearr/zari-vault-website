# Zari Vault - Off-Page SEO & Backlink Acquisition Plan

Companion to `astro-seo-plan.md`. On-page/content (Phases 1-4) is largely built:
Astro site, a 12-post blog cluster, and programmatic country pages. This plan
covers **off-page** work: earning the backlinks and brand signals that make that
content rank. Backlinks are earned, never bought, and compound over months.

## Goal

Build a steady flow of relevant, credible inbound links to grow domain
authority and referral traffic, which converts to app installs. Target output:
**2-4 quality links per month**, prioritising relevance over volume.

## Guardrails (read first)

- **Never buy links, use link farms, or do mass automated outreach.** Google
  penalises this and it can bury the whole domain.
- **Relevance beats volume.** One link from a respected SA tech/consumer outlet
  is worth more than fifty directory links.
- **Only claim what is true.** No fake reviews, no "official/verified/POPIA
  compliant" claims until confirmed (see website brief). Misleading claims are a
  reputational and legal risk.
- **Track everything** so effort compounds (see the tracker at the end).

## Tier 1 - Launch quick wins (do first, low effort)

App directories and listings. These are legitimate, give real referral traffic,
and are appropriate for a launched app:

- **Product Hunt** launch (pick a Tuesday-Thursday; line up a few supporters).
- App-review and discovery sites: AlternativeTo, Slant, SaaSHub, Apptopia,
  app-of-the-day style newsletters.
- **South African startup / tech directories**: SA Startup Index, Ventureburn /
  Memeburn startup listings, Disrupt Africa directory, SiliconCape community,
  ZA-focused Betalist-style sites.
- Privacy-tool roundups and "privacy-first app" lists (the no-upload angle fits).
- Your own owned profiles: a Crunchbase/LinkedIn company page, Play Store and App
  Store listings linking back to zarivault.co.za.

## Tier 2 - Digital PR (highest value, medium effort)

The privacy / POPIA / "your ID unlocks your whole life" angle is a genuine hook
for SA media.

- **Pitch SA tech & consumer press**: MyBroadband, ITWeb, Stuff South Africa,
  TechCentral, BusinessTech, Ventureburn, htxt.africa. Angle options:
  - Founder story (mugged, lost ID and licence, built the fix). Human, local.
  - "Is a digital copy of your ID legal in SA?" explainer (repurpose the blog
    post as an op-ed / expert comment).
  - Identity-fraud / document-safety commentary around news hooks (SAPS stats,
    Home Affairs backlogs, data-breach stories).
- **HARO / Qwoto / SA journalist requests**: respond as an identity-security
  source to earn contextual links.
- **Offer the founder as an expert** to fintech/security podcasts and YouTube
  channels in the SA ecosystem.

## Tier 3 - Partnerships & community (ongoing)

Organisations whose audiences overlap, where a link back is natural:

- **Driving schools** (licence-renewal content), **estate agents / managing
  agents**, **universities & student bodies** (student card, FICA for first bank
  accounts), **insurers & brokers** (document organisation), **immigration /
  relocation consultants** (the country pages are directly relevant).
- Co-marketing: guest posts, "recommended tools" pages, resource links.
- Local community: SiliconCape, Heavy Chef, startup Slack/WhatsApp groups,
  university entrepreneurship societies.

## Tier 4 - Link-worthy assets (build once, earn links for years)

Give people a reason to link without asking:

- The **document guides** already published (keep expanding the cluster).
- A **free tool**: e.g. a "when does my licence/passport expire?" reminder
  calculator, or a downloadable "SA document renewal checklist" PDF.
- **Original data / survey**: poll South Africans on document loss, renewal
  frustration, or fraud experience, then publish the stats. Journalists link to
  original numbers.
- The **country pages** as a reference for "identity document by country".

## 90-day cadence

- **Weeks 1-2**: submit every Tier 1 listing; set up the tracker; prep the
  Product Hunt launch assets.
- **Weeks 3-4**: Product Hunt launch; begin Tier 2 media list + first 5 pitches.
- **Month 2**: 2 pitches/week; start HARO responses; line up 1-2 partnerships;
  ship one link-worthy asset (checklist PDF or survey).
- **Month 3**: publish survey/data; guest post(s); review what earned links and
  double down. Re-pitch non-responders once.

## Outreach templates

**Media pitch (founder story):**
> Subject: SA founder built a private ID vault after being mugged
>
> Hi [name], I'm Miense, founder of Zari Vault, a South African app that keeps
> your ID, licence and passport encrypted on your own phone (never uploaded).
> I built it after I was mugged and lost my documents. With Home Affairs
> backlogs and rising identity fraud, I think your readers would find the
> privacy angle useful. Happy to share the story, data, or expert comment on
> document safety. Would that be of interest?

**Partnership / resource link:**
> Subject: A free resource for your [members/clients]
>
> Hi [name], we publish plain-language guides on SA documents (Smart ID,
> licence renewal, FICA) that your [students/residents/clients] often ask
> about. Free, no sign-up. If useful, a link from your resources page is
> welcome, and we're happy to write a guest piece tailored to your audience.

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet (Google Sheet or Notion): Target | Type (directory/PR/
partner) | Contact | Date reached out | Status | Link URL | DA/relevance notes |
Follow-up date. Review monthly; drop what doesn't work, repeat what does.

## What success looks like

- Months 1-3: foundation links (directories, Product Hunt, first press).
- Months 3-6: steady editorial + partnership links; blog/country pages start
  ranking for long-tail queries.
- Months 6-12: compounding authority; branded search growth; installs from
  organic + referral become a measurable channel.

SEO is a months-long compounding game. The content machine is built; this is the
demand side that makes it rank.
