Our Story

We built the app we needed ourselves

🔐

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Most document apps upload your most sensitive information to servers owned by companies you've never heard of. We built Zari Vault to be architecturally different — your documents never leave your device because our infrastructure literally cannot receive them.

Zari Vault was born from a simple frustration: fumbling through a wallet at an estate boom gate, failing to find an expired licence at a roadblock, scrambling for a passport number during online check-in. Identity documents are the most important cards in your life — yet we treat them like an afterthought.

We designed Zari Vault to feel like the app that actually lives on your home screen. Fast, clean, and completely private. No account to create, no email to verify, no data to hand over. Just scan your document, lock the vault, and it's there when you need it.

POPIA compliant by architecture, not by checkbox. South Africa's data protection law demands better from every app that handles your personal information. Zari Vault was built to exceed that standard.

🏠

Privacy by architecture

We can't access your data because our system is built to never receive it. That's not a promise — it's a technical fact.

⚡

Speed in the real world

Every UI decision is made with one question: can you show your document before the security guard loses patience?

🇿🇦

Built for Africa, used everywhere

Boom gates, roadblocks, FICA compliance — designed around real South African daily friction, with global reach.
