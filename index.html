The reality

Your ID number unlocks your entire life

Bank accounts, credit, SIM registration, property. Once an ID number leaks, fraudsters use it for years, opening accounts and taking loans in your name long after the breach.

Cloud risk

Every server upload is a potential breach

When your documents live on a server, they're exposed to insider threats, hackers, and government orders, regardless of what a privacy policy says. No server means no breach surface.

Your POPIA rights

South Africans have strong data protections

Under POPIA, you have the right to know what data is collected about you, to access it, correct it, or delete it. Any app storing your documents is a data processor, and must notify you of breaches.

His conviction around data sovereignty is personal. As someone who has had his own data used against him, and who watched AI-driven exploitation make ordinary people increasingly vulnerable, Katleho understands viscerally why most people hate having their information harvested and sold for ad revenue.
