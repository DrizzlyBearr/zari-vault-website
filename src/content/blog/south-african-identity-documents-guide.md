Your identity documents run your life quietly in the background: proving who you are, letting you drive, travel, bank and get things done. This guide brings together everything South Africans need to know about their key documents, with links to detailed how-to articles for each one. Bookmark it as your starting point.

## Your core identity document: the Smart ID

The Smart ID card is South Africa's modern identity document, replacing the old green ID book with a secure, chip-based card.

- New to it, or turning 16? See [how to apply for a Smart ID card](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-smart-id-card-in-south-africa).
- Still have the old book? See [green ID book vs Smart ID card](/blog/green-id-book-vs-smart-id-card).
- Lost or stolen? See [how to replace a lost ID and get a temporary ID](/blog/lost-id-how-to-replace-it-south-africa).

## Driving documents

Two separate things are easy to confuse, and both matter:

- Your **driving licence** card, valid five years. See [driver's licence renewal](/blog/drivers-licence-renewal-south-africa).
- Your **vehicle licence disc**, renewed yearly. See [how to renew your vehicle licence disc](/blog/vehicle-licence-disc-renewal-south-africa).
- Getting stopped? See [what documents to carry at a roadblock](/blog/what-documents-to-carry-at-a-roadblock-south-africa).

## Travel

- Your **passport**, valid ten years for adults. See [passport application and renewal](/blog/passport-application-and-renewal-south-africa), and remember to check the validity and blank-page rules for your destination.
