Losing your ID is stressful, partly because of the admin and partly because of the risk. Your ID is the key to your bank account and your identity, so a lost or stolen card is something to act on quickly. Here is a calm, step-by-step plan.

## Step 1: Protect yourself first

Before the replacement admin, reduce the risk of someone misusing your identity:

- If your ID was **stolen**, report it to the **police** so there is a record.
- Consider alerting your **bank** so they can watch for suspicious activity.
- You can also register a protective alert with the **Southern African Fraud Prevention Service (SAFPS)**, a free service that flags your identity to member institutions.

Being quick here is the best protection against identity fraud. Our guide on [keeping your documents safe](/blog/how-to-keep-your-identity-documents-safe) covers the warning signs to watch for.

## Step 2: Apply for a replacement Smart ID

A lost ID is replaced by applying for a **re-issue** of your Smart ID card. You can do this:

- Online via the **eHomeAffairs** portal (ehome.dha.gov.za), then book a slot, or
- At a **Home Affairs office** or a **participating bank branch**.

A **re-issue fee** applies (fees change, so confirm the current amount on dha.gov.za). The process is the same biometric capture as a normal Smart ID application, which we cover in [how to apply for a Smart ID card](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-smart-id-card-in-south-africa).

## Step 3: Get a Temporary Identity Certificate if you need one

If you need proof of identity urgently while you wait for the new card, Home Affairs can issue a **Temporary Identity Certificate (TIC)**. It is a short-validity document that stands in for your ID for many purposes. You apply for it in person at a DHA office, and a small fee usually applies.

## What to bring

- Any supporting details you have of your ID number
- Proof of address may be requested
- The applicable fees

## Avoid the next scramble

The worst part of losing an ID is not remembering your own ID number when you need it most.
